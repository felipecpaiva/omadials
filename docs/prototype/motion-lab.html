omadials motion lab

Six ways for the corner to open and close, on identical desktops. Every style opens out of the tab and closes back into it. Tap a corner, or press in it and drag outward: each style follows your finger and finishes from your flick. Tune speed and bounce, then pick the one that feels right.

Pick a favourite with its "Pick this" button; tell me the name and your speed and bounce, and that motion goes into the plugin. Numbers come from docs/motion-research.md. Springs use mass 1, stiffness (2π / duration)² and damping ratio 1 − bounce, as Apple describes them.

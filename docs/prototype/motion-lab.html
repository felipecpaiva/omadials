omadials motion lab

Seven ways for the corner to open and close, on identical desktops. Tap a corner to open or close it, or press in the corner and drag outward: the radius styles follow your finger and finish from your flick. Tune speed and bounce, then pick the one that feels right.

Pick a favourite with its "Pick this" button; tell me the name and your speed and bounce, and that motion goes into the plugin. Numbers come from docs/motion-research.md. Springs use mass 1, stiffness (2π / duration)² and damping ratio 1 − bounce, as Apple describes them.

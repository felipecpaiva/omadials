# omadials brand brief

The brief behind every logo variation. Written 2026-11-17, before any image was made, so the variations can be
judged against it and not against taste alone. Same shape as the omagnet and OmaBar briefs.

## What the project is, in one line

A control corner for Omarchy: tap or push into a screen corner and a quarter dial opens with your hardware quick
settings (brightness, volume, mic as arcs you drag, toggles for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, night light and more).

## Who it is for

Omarchy users on laptops and tablets (touch first, mouse fine) who want their quick settings one gesture away
instead of spread across bar widgets. Terminal-comfortable people who judge a project by its logo and its first
screenshot in a few seconds.

## What the brand has to say

1. **Turn, don't hunt.** Settings are arcs you sweep with a thumb, not menus you dig through.
2. **From the corner.** It lives in a screen corner and grows out of it.
3. **At home on Omarchy.** Looks like it belongs beside Omarchy's own apps and themes, without pretending to be
   official.
4. **Small and precise.** Sibling of omagnet, omahop and OmaBar by the same maker.

## Name

`omadials`, all lower case, like omagnet and omahop. `oma` is the community's Omarchy prefix; `dials` is the
interaction (plural: there are several concentric dials). Say it "oh-ma-dials". Plugin id:
`io.github.felipecpaiva.omadials`.

## The idea to draw from

The product's own look: a quarter circle anchored in a corner, with 2 or 3 concentric arcs, each partly filled and
ending in a round knob, and a few small round toggles on an inner arc. The core action is dragging a knob along an
arc.

## References, and what not to take from them

| Reference | What to learn from it | What must NOT be copied |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Omarchy** (`/usr/share/omarchy/logo.svg`, `icon.png`) | Chunky pixel blocks, one bright colour on a plain ground, monospace. | Its logo or icon, or anything that reads as an official Omarchy mark. Omarchy has not endorsed this project. |
| **Phone quick settings, smart-home dials, analog gauges** | The idea of a dial you turn. | Any vendor's icon, name or styling. |
| **Family: omagnet, omahop, OmaBar** (`../../omagnet/branding/`, `../../OmaBarConfigurator/branding/`) | Graphite ground, one accent, JetBrains Mono, works at 16 px. | Their marks. omadials needs its own silhouette (OmaBar is pills on a rail, omagnet is a snap grid). |

Names appear only as plain text, with the disclaimer in the README: not affiliated with or endorsed by Omarchy.

## Directions to explore (3)

- **v1-quadrant:** a quarter circle in the bottom-left of the tile, 2 to 3 concentric arcs, the outer one filled
  partway with a knob at its end. The product, simplified.
- **v2-knob:** a single bold arc with one round knob, like a volume dial seen from the front. One gesture.
- **v3-monogram:** a lower-case `d` (or `o`) whose bowl is drawn as concentric arcs. The name in an initial.

## Constraints every variation must meet

- **Works small.** Readable at 16 and 32 pixels, in one colour. Arcs must not blur into a blob at 16 px.
- **Works on light and dark.** A one-colour version for each (Omarchy themes "White" and "Tokyo Night").
- **No gradients needed.** The mark stands without gradient, shadow or glow.
- **Vector first.** A real SVG source for the icon and the wordmark; PNG exports come from it.
- **Own idea.** Different from Omarchy's, omagnet's and OmaBar's marks at a glance, even as a thumbnail. Not a
  generic "settings gear".
- **Text rules.** No em dash or en dash anywhere; plain English.
- **Licence.** Made for this project; released under the project's MIT licence unless the maintainer chooses otherwise.

## Deliverables for the chosen variation

1. Icon: `icon.svg`, PNG at 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, 16; one-colour light and dark; a monochrome mask.
2. Wordmark and horizontal lockup, on light, dark and transparent.
3. GitHub social preview, 1280 by 640, under 1 MB.
4. README banner with the tagline.
5. Marketplace `preview.png` at the repo root.
6. Terminal banner in plain text (half-block letters).
7. Demo GIFs per feature, screenshots of the panel (after the plugin exists).
8. One-page brand sheet: palette with contrast ratios, type, spacing, do and do not.

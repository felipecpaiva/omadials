# The dial d

A lowercase d has an upright stem and a circular bowl. A second concentric, open arc inside the bowl turns the initial into plural dials.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #c2a0ff; deep accent #7041ac. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 8.26:1 (minimum 3:1); deep on paper 6.22:1 (minimum 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: curved dial geometry differs from Omarchy's chunky pixel mark, omagnet's four-part snap grid and OmaBar's horizontal pills on a rail. This direction uses an asymmetric lowercase d with a concentric arc counter.

At 16 px: The 2 px outer bowl and stem carry the lowercase d silhouette. The inner arc is 1.4 px wide with a 1.3 px radial gap. It remains a separate arc in one colour.

Typeface: lowercase omadials in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original paths and circular arcs on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.

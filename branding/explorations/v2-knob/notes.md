# One gesture

A single bold open dial ends in a cyan round knob. The empty centre and open lower sector focus the mark on one sweeping gesture.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #52d9e8; deep accent #006b78. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 10.53:1 (minimum 3:1); deep on paper 5.61:1 (minimum 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: curved dial geometry differs from Omarchy's chunky pixel mark, omagnet's four-part snap grid and OmaBar's horizontal pills on a rail. This direction uses one open circular sweep with a knob.

At 16 px: The 2.4 px stroke and broad empty centre hold at 16 px. A 3.6 px knob makes the endpoint wider than the arc. The lower opening stays clear in one colour.

Typeface: lowercase omadials in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original paths and circular arcs on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.

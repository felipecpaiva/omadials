# From the corner

Two quarter arcs grow from a bottom-left anchor. A coral outer sweep ends in a round drag knob; two inner dots suggest toggles.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #ff8c78; deep accent #a83e2c. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 7.84:1 (minimum 3:1); deep on paper 5.58:1 (minimum 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: curved dial geometry differs from Omarchy's chunky pixel mark, omagnet's four-part snap grid and OmaBar's horizontal pills on a rail. This direction uses a corner-anchored quarter fan.

At 16 px: The 1.8 px rings leave 2.2 px radial gaps. The two 2 px toggles stay separate from the inner ring. The knob adds a local bulge, even in one colour.

Typeface: lowercase omadials in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original paths and circular arcs on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.
